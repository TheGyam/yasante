## Other observations

1. **UIAutomator Dump Idle Timeouts:** Throughout the test run across all three emulators, screens with ongoing animations (e.g. animated pulsing badges, loader skeletons, or Reanimated transitions) intermittently caused `uiautomator dump` to fail with `ERROR: could not get idle state`. While `qadb find` and text-matching `tapon` commands depend on this dump, coordinate taps (`qadb tap <x> <y>`) execute immediately and reliably via ADB input.
2. **Move on Non-Working Days (Step M5):** When client2 requested a move of B2 to Monday 28 September (a day the stylist does not work), both the move request (`POST /move/`) and stylist acceptance (`POST /move/answer/`) succeeded with HTTP 200. The backend does not currently validate stylist working-day schedules when moving an existing booking.
3. **Waitlist Notification Logs (Step X3):** When B6 was cancelled >48h, Celery task `apps.common.notifications.send_push` immediately dispatched and logged a notification in `NotificationLog` with `category: "waitlist"`. The notification body `Thu 1 Oct. Ask before someone else does.` and alert banner `A slot opened with Braided by Bisi / Thu 1 Oct` were fully rendered on client2's screen. Because emulators register no Expo push tokens, the notification status in the DB was recorded as `no_device`, which bypassed the `logger.info("Push (log only) ...")` log line in `server.log`.
4. **Deposit Field & Accept Flow (Step N1):** Clearing the deposit input (`Deposit to confirm`) causes the payment method selector and bank transfer details to disappear, leaving the `Accept` button to immediately confirm the booking without deposit (`deposit: null`).
5. **Contest Protection (Steps C5 & N3):** Both timing records and no-show marks can be contested exactly once by the client. In both flows, the contest button disappeared immediately upon submission, and subsequent API POST attempts were rejected with HTTP 409 Conflict (`"You’ve already contested it"`).
6. **No Reviews After No-Show (Step N4):** When an appointment is closed out as a no-show, the `Leave a review` button and `booking-review` testID are completely omitted from the client's screen, preventing no-show reviews.
