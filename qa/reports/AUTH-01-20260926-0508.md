## Deviations & Notes

- **Email address entry anomaly (Client email typo):**
  In Step B7, the client account was registered with email `a01.0508.client@example.come.dev` instead of `a01.0508.client@example.com` due to leftover trailing `.dev` characters from the prior duplicate email check input (`ama.achieng@yasante.dev`). This address was subsequently maintained and used consistently across all client verification, login, and password reset steps throughout Parts B and C.

- **Universal link resolution & custom scheme protocol (Finding F2):**
  In Steps C3 and C4, Android activity manager refused to resolve universal HTTPS links (`https://yasante.com/auth/reset-password...`) directly to `com.yasante.app` because the pre-built development client APK (`base.apk`) only registers custom schemes `yasante://` and `exp+yasante://`. As documented in Finding F2, deep links were dispatched and tested using the custom scheme `yasante:///auth/reset-password...`.

- **UI tree availability & uiautomator dump timeouts:**
  Across 30 steps with dynamic animations, transitions, or blinking cursors (particularly the `CodeInput` screens across P5, B7, B8, B9, B10, B11, B12, BL1, BL2, BL3, BL4, D2, D3, D4), `uiautomator dump` encountered idle state timeouts (`ERROR: could not get idle state.`), resulting in `UI tree unavailable` and empty 1-byte `visible.txt` files. This is an expected operational characteristic documented in `qa/AGENTS.md` §6; full visual verification was established via `screen.png` and corroborated by backend server logs.
