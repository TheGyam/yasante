# Online Safety Act assessments

**Approved by Yaw on 21 October 2026.** Written that day from what the code does, and
not legal advice: a lawyer should still look it over. The law
expects these assessments to be kept up to date: review them at least once a year, and
before any big change, such as adding private messages, opening to under-18s or
launching in a new country.

## 1. Why Yasante is covered

The UK Online Safety Act 2023 covers user-to-user services: anywhere people can post
something that other people can see. On Yasante, people post looks (photos with
captions), comments, reviews, stylist pages and profiles, and these are seen by other
people in the app and on yasante.com. Yasante is aimed at people in the UK, so the
Act applies.

The Act asks every covered service to:

1. assess the risk of illegal content and act on what the assessment finds;
2. assess whether children can use the service, and if so, assess and reduce the risks
   to them;
3. let people report content and complain, and deal with reports quickly;
4. explain all of this in the terms of use;
5. name a person accountable for it, and keep written records.

Ofcom's codes say what's expected. A small, low-risk service like Yasante has to do
less than a large one, but the steps above apply to everyone.

## 3. How Yasante works, as far as safety goes

| Feature | Who sees it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Looks: a photo, caption, styles and hair type | Everyone, in the app and on yasante.com | Can be reported, hidden or removed |
| Comments and replies on looks | Everyone who can see the look | Can be reported, hidden or removed |
| Reviews of stylists | Everyone, on the stylist's page | Only after a completed appointment; checked before publishing |
| Stylist pages, with photos of their work | Everyone | Phone and proof-of-work checks behind a verified badge |
| Profiles: name, username and photo | Everyone | Can be reported |
| Booking requests, with a short note | Only the client and the stylist | Not public; tied to a real appointment |
| Private messages | **None.** Yasante has no direct messages or chat | |
| Shared collections and care plans (planned, docs/SHARING_PLAN.md): a name, looks or steps, and the author's name, by link | Anyone with the link; never searchable | Can be reported; staff can stop the sharing |
| Live video, groups, file sharing | **None** | |

Things already in place:

- **Reporting** from the app on any look, comment, review, stylist or person. The
  reasons are "Not their work", "Not about hair", "Nudity", "Hate", "Scam" and
  "Other". Reports go to a queue on the staff site.
- **Moderation**, done by a person: content can be hidden or removed, and stylist pages
  unpublished. Every decision is logged. The author is told why, and can appeal from
  the app.
- **Blocking**: blocked accounts disappear from each other's feeds and can't comment on
  each other's posts.
- **Reviews** are moderated before they're published, and only allowed after a
  completed appointment.
- **Copied photos**: new looks and portfolio photos are compared with photos already
  on Yasante, and near-copies are flagged for a person to check.
- **Usernames and handles** are checked against a list of words that aren't allowed.
- **Photos** have their location data stripped. Proof-of-work photos are private.
- **Limits** on how fast people can post, comment and report.
- **Age**: everyone confirms they're 18 or over at sign-up, and the terms say so.

## 4. Illegal content risk assessment

Ofcom lists 17 kinds of priority illegal content. For each, the table gives the risk on
Yasante, the reason, and what reduces it. "Low" means the features that carry this
harm are missing or very limited.

| Kind of illegal content | Risk | Why | What reduces it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Terrorism | Low | Posts must be hair photos; no groups, live video or file sharing | Reports, moderation, "Not about hair" removal |
| Child sexual exploitation and abuse: grooming | Low | No private messages; booking notes only between a client and a stylist with a real appointment; 18+ only | Reports, blocking, moderation |
| Child sexual abuse material | Low | Photos are public and about hair; no private sharing | Reports ("Nudity"), moderation. See the gaps below |
| Hate | Medium | Comments and captions are open text; hair is closely tied to race and identity | "Hate" report reason, moderation, blocking |
| Harassment, stalking, threats and abuse | Medium | Comments, reviews and the stylist–client relationship | Reporting, blocking, the stylist's address shown only once a booking is confirmed |
| Controlling or coercive behaviour | Low | No private messages | Blocking |
| Intimate image abuse | Low | Photos are of hair, public and moderated | "Nudity" reports, removal |
| Extreme pornography | Low | As above | As above |
| Sexual exploitation of adults | Low | No private messages or paid content | Reports, moderation |
| Human trafficking | Low | Stylists' services are hair only; prices and places are public | Stylist checks, reports |
| Unlawful immigration | Low | Nothing on Yasante helps with this | Reports |
| Fraud and financial services | Medium | Deposits are paid straight to stylists, so a fake stylist could take deposits | Phone and proof-of-work checks, the verified badge, "Scam" reports, reviews only after real appointments, terms that forbid asking for payment outside the agreed deposit |
| Proceeds of crime | Low | Yasante doesn't handle money | — |
| Drugs and psychoactive substances | Low | Posts are about hair; the product shelf only takes hair products | Reports, moderation |
| Firearms, knives and other weapons | Low | As above | As above |
| Encouraging or assisting suicide or self-harm | Low | No private messages; comments are public | Reports, moderation |
| Foreign interference | Low | No political content or advertising | Reports |
| Animal cruelty | Low | As above | Reports |

**Overall: low risk, with medium risk for hate, harassment and deposit fraud.** The
biggest single reason the risk is low is that Yasante has no private messages. Adding
them would need a new assessment first.

## 5. Children's access assessment

The question is whether children can use Yasante, and if so, whether many do or it's
likely to attract them.

- **Can children use it?** Yes, in practice. Sign-up asks people to confirm they're 18
  or over, but Ofcom doesn't count a tick-box like this as reliable age checking.
  Looks and stylist pages can also be seen on yasante.com without an account.
- **Is it likely to attract children?** Probably. Hairstyle photos appeal to teenagers
  as much as to adults, even though the bookings and the terms are for adults.

**Conclusion: treat Yasante as likely to be used by children.** Yaw decided on 21
October 2026 to keep the age check as it is (people confirm they're 18 or over) and to
apply the children's risk assessment below. If reliable age checks are added later,
such as an age estimate from a selfie or an ID check, look at this again.

### Children's risk assessment

| Harm to children | Risk | Why | What reduces it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pornography | Low | Hair photos, moderated; nudity can be reported | "Nudity" reports, removal |
| Suicide, self-harm and eating disorders | Low | Not the subject of the service; comments are public | Reports, moderation |
| Bullying and abuse | Medium | Comments on someone's hair or looks | Reports, blocking, moderation |
| Body image and appearance pressure | Medium | Feeds of styled hair; hair is personal and tied to identity | No beauty filters or face editing; "Not for me" lets people see less of what they don't want |

## 7. Not covered here

Copyright (a stolen photo) isn't covered by the Online Safety Act. It's handled by the
"Not their work" report and the copy check. If Yasante opens in the US, register a
DMCA agent with the US Copyright Office.
