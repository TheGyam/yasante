# Imagery and social

## Photography

- Real people, real hair and skin, shot close and warm. Hair texture and skin tone are the subject: never smoothed away, never lightened.
- Dark, low-key backgrounds that sit naturally on `bg`; warm rim light picks up the brand's pinks and ambers.
- Stylists' own photos in the feed stay as they shot them. Don't add filters or brand overlays to people's work.
- No stock-photo smiles at the camera, no flat-lay clutter.

## Brand light

Brand surfaces (splash, store art, social, the website hero) may carry soft glows: rose (`glow`, low left) and amber (`gold`, high right) at 10–30% opacity on `bg` dark. The icon may carry `shadow-icon-glow`. Glows never sit behind body text.

## Social

- Profile picture: the app icon master, cropped to the circle with the mark centred.
- Posts: 1080×1350. `bg` dark, one idea per post, the mark or a photo as the hero, the name in the `wordmark` style, at most one line of type besides.
- Captions follow the voice, lowercase allowed, one emoji at most, a few hashtags (`#yasante`).
- Stories and reels: the logo reveal as the opener or end card.

## App store

The icon master on `bg` with the glow; screenshots show real screens in dark mode first, a single sentence of `title2` above each, sentence case, no device frames needed.
