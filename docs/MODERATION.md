# Handling reports

For whoever works the report queues on the staff site. Caleb Kyei is accountable for
this (see [ONLINE_SAFETY.md](ONLINE_SAFETY.md)). Written 21 October 2026.

## Where reports come from

- **In the app**: anyone can report a look, comment, review, stylist page or person.
  They land in the staff site under **Reports** and **Review reports**.
- **By email**: anyone, with or without an account, can write to support@yasante.com.
  Find the content they mean and act on it the same way. Reply to them.
- **Appeals**: when we hide or remove something, its author can appeal from the app.
  Appeals land under **Appeals**.

Reports marked **"Something illegal, or that puts someone in danger"** are always at
the top of the queue.

## How fast

| What | Aim |
| --- | --- |
| Illegal or dangerous | Look the same day, within a few hours where possible |
| Every other report | Within 24 hours, as the terms promise |
| Appeals | Within 7 days, by someone other than whoever made the decision, where possible |
| Complaints by email | A reply within 14 days, as the terms promise |

## The three decisions

- **Hide (h)**: takes it out of view while the author can still see it and appeal.
  Use it when you're unsure, or when the author could fix it.
- **Remove (r)**: takes it down for good. Use it when it clearly breaks the terms or
  the law.
- **Dismiss (d)**: leaves it up. Use it when it doesn't break the rules, even if
  someone dislikes it.

Each decision is logged with who made it and when. The author can see in the app
what happened and why, and can appeal. Add a short note saying why. It helps whoever handles the
appeal.

## What to do, by kind of report

| Report | Usually | Also |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not their work | Hide, then check the near-copy flags under **Flagged photos** | Remove if it's clearly someone else's photo |
| Not about hair | Hide or remove | — |
| Nudity or sexual content | Remove | If it could involve anyone under 18, follow "Child safety" below |
| Hateful or abusive | Remove if it attacks someone for who they are, including their race or hair; dismiss a plain bad opinion | Close the account of anyone who keeps doing it |
| A scam | Remove, and look at the account's other posts | Unpublish a stylist page that takes deposits and doesn't deliver; tell the person to report it to Report Fraud |
| Shares private details (reviews) | Remove | — |
| Appointment didn't happen (reviews) | Check the booking; remove if it wasn't completed | — |
| Illegal or dangerous | See below | — |

## Illegal or dangerous

1. **Someone is in danger right now** (a threat to hurt someone, or a risk of
   suicide): call **999**. Then remove the content.
2. **Child safety**, such as a sexual image of anyone who could be under 18, or an
   adult trying to contact a child:
   - **Don't download, screenshot, copy or forward the image.** Even holding a copy
     can be an offence.
   - Remove it from view with **Remove** straight away.
   - Report it to the **Internet Watch Foundation** at report.iwf.org.uk. If a child
     is at risk, also report to **CEOP** at ceop.police.uk.
   - Close the account that posted it.
   - Write down what you did, and when, in the review note. Don't describe the image.
   - Don't delete the account's data until the police or the IWF say so. They may
     need it.
3. **Terrorist content**: remove it and report it at gov.uk/report-terrorism.
4. **Fraud**: remove it, and report it at reportfraud.police.uk (in Scotland, call
   Police Scotland on 101).
5. **Other crimes**: remove it, and report non-emergencies to the police on 101.

Never contact the person who posted illegal content to warn them first.

## Keeping records

The staff site logs every decision automatically. For anything sent to the police,
the IWF or another authority, add a review note with the date and any reference number
they give you. Caleb reviews the queues and these notes each month, and the
assessment in [ONLINE_SAFETY.md](ONLINE_SAFETY.md) at least once a year.
